Core SOC Responsibilities

Responsibility Description
Identify Assets Gaining holistic understanding of tools and technologies at disposal. Learning about hardware and software running on systems. In-depth understanding helps in early detection of potential cyber threats and existing vulnerabilities.
Proactive Monitoring Primary focus on detecting malicious activities on the network before they can lead to substantial harm. Real-time security monitoring and alerting.
Manage Logs, Configuration & Response Thorough management of activity logs for forensic investigation. Tracing back to the point where something may have gone wrong during security incidents.
Rank Alerts by Severity Detecting threats and irregularities while ranking severity of incidents. Prioritizing response to events based on criticality and impact.
Adjust Defenses Vulnerability management and increasing awareness about threats. Helping the team stay vigilant for security breaches and emerging threats.
Check Compliance Ensuring the organization complies with applicable regulations and standards. Maintaining security posture and regulatory requirements.
Notify on Security Breach Alerting stakeholders as quickly as possible to ensure business continuity. Minimizing network downtime during unexpected security incidents.
